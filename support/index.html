Frequently asked questions

I lost my progress

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes it. If you changed device or reinstalled the game, write to us with the game name and we will do our best to help.

The game crashes or does not start

Update the game to the latest version from the store and restart your device. If it still happens, send us your device model and OS version.

Ads

Our games are free and supported by ads. Rewarded videos are always optional. If an ad does not close or shows something inappropriate, tell us which game and when it happened.

Notifications

You can turn reminders on or off at any time in your device settings (Settings › Notifications › the game).

Delete my data

Write to us from the email above with the game name: we answer every privacy request.
